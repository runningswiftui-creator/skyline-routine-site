QUESTIONS

A few things people ask.

Is this a subscription?

No. Skyline Routine is free to download and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Which systems are supported?

macOS 13 or later on the Mac App Store. A Windows direct license is also available for Windows 10/11 x64.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Reminders are stored locally and the app works offline. An internet connection is only needed for the initial purchase.

Where is my data stored?

Reminder content and settings are stored on your computer. We do not operate a cloud account system or upload reminder data.

How do I get the app after buying?

Download Skyline Routine free from the Mac App Store. Open Settings → Upgrade to Pro to purchase the optional one-time upgrade.

What is the refund policy?

Mac App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Windows direct purchases and refunds are handled by Lemon Squeezy. See our terms for details.
