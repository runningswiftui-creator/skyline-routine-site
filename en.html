DESKTOP FLIGHT REMINDERS

A reminder that
actually gets noticed.

Skyline Routine does not disappear after a few seconds. When a reminder is due, a plane takes off and keeps cruising until you click it or the reminder text. You can also set a maximum flight time so it lands automatically when you are away.

Visit product page Copy share links

What it is for

  • Drinking water, standing up, and screen breaks
  • Medicine, meetings, appointments, and leaving work
  • Pomodoro, stretching, meditation, and eye rest
  • Any routine that should be seen and acknowledged

What makes it different

A normal notification vanishes in seconds. Skyline Routine uses a persistent plane flight to make the reminder impossible to ignore. Click the plane or the reminder text to end the flight.

Features

  • Daily, weekdays, once, and interval reminders
  • 18 aircraft styles including bomber and reconnaissance, plus custom images
  • 6 reminder card styles — 2 free, all 6 with Pro; per-reminder overrides
  • First-run quick start and one-click scene packs
  • Snooze, skip today, gentle mode, meeting mode, and pause with countdown
  • Next reminder in the menu bar
  • Flight area: full screen, quarters, drag-to-resize custom, per reminder
  • Optional cursor avoidance with near / medium / far distances
  • No runway or black mask; short 5-second landing notice
  • Weekly share card
  • Local backup export and import
  • Custom flight paths — 1 free, unlimited with Pro
  • Pro themes: Aurora, Sunset, Midnight, 1024, Candy, Anime
  • Pause all when 5+ reminders are enabled
  • Multiple interval time templates
  • 14 languages, light/dark mode, auto-landing

Local by design

No account and no cloud upload. Reminder data and custom images stay on your computer. macOS 13+ on the Mac App Store; Windows 10/11 x64 direct license is also available.

Free download + Pro

Free includes 3 active reminders, 4 aircraft, 2 card styles, and one custom path. Pro is a one-time upgrade with unlimited reminders, all 18 aircraft, all 6 card styles, unlimited paths, custom images, and 6 Pro themes.

Suggested price: $4.99 / ¥30 regular, $2.99 / ¥18 launch.
