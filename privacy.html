Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Skyline Routine is designed to work locally. This policy explains what the application and this website do with your information.

Reminder data

Reminder text, schedules, settings, and optional custom aircraft images are stored on your computer. Skyline Routine does not operate a cloud account system and does not upload reminder content to a server.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy receives the information required to process payment, tax, refunds, and license delivery. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.

Website

This website does not include advertising or third-party analytics. Standard hosting logs may be processed by the hosting provider for security and reliability.

Support

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information in support messages.

Contact

Privacy questions: runningswiftui@gmail.com
